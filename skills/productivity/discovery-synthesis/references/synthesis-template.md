# Synthesis Template

Use this structure for the artifact at the `discovery` key path.

```md
# Discovery — <topic>

**Sources:** <n> (<breakdown by type>), <date range>
**Question this evidence was gathered to answer:** <one sentence, or "not stated">

## Source Inventory

| Id | Type | Date | Who (as stated, redacted) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I-01 | interview | <date> | <role / segment> | <length, context> |

## Findings

Ordered by evidence strength.

### F1 — <job name>

**Job:** When <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I can <outcome>.
**Evidence:** <n> of <total> sources — <ids>
**Pains:**
- (3) <pain> — <ids>
- (2) <pain> — <ids>
**Desired outcomes:** <what they said would mean done>
**Workarounds today:** <what they do instead> — <ids>
**Quotes:**
> "<verbatim>" — I-03
> "<verbatim>" — T-117

## Anecdotes

Single-source observations, same shape as a finding, unranked.

## Contradictions

Where sources disagree: the claim, each side, and the ids behind each.

## Gaps

What this evidence could not surface — segments not represented, questions not asked, situations not covered.

## Hypotheses to Test

Guesses about cause or possible solutions, each tied to the finding it came from. These are not findings.
```
