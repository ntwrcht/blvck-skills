Produces a PRD with a problem statement, solution, success metrics, user stories, and an out-of-scope section. It keeps every fact the prompt gives: one-at-a-time refunds today, 1,840 refunds and 37 duplicates during the March outage, a 200-order limit, team-lead approval above $500 per order, a CSV export for finance, and partial refunds out of scope. The single-refund time baseline is written as UNKNOWN. It presents no invented detail as given fact: a number, date, name, or customer claim the prompt does not supply appears only when labelled as a proposal, assumption, open question, or UNKNOWN, or when it is arithmetic on the given numbers.
